# Ridgewell design direction

## Owner direction

The owner rejected the earlier white page with isometric vector drawings, and then two text-only layouts (a bento grid and a sticky split), because none of them showed the software Ridgewell actually ships. The approved direction is "Showroom": a product-forward page in which drawn product screens inside browser and phone frames carry the argument. The current palette uses the owner's requested white canvas and white panels, with Barlow Condensed and Manrope, and Ridgewell red as the only accent. The owner also asked that clients stay unnamed, that copy stay at outcome level so competitors cannot read the build recipe, and that the LeaseTrack building be labelled "Condominium". The approved concept spec is in `docs/superpowers/specs/`.

## Context

Ridgewell Management Services, Inc. offers two services: Website design and Bespoke system development. It started in accounting in 2004, so the books and the workflow come first. Visitors are Philippine SME owners and school or property administrators. The page has three jobs: make the two services clear in one screen, show seven systems in daily use (five built for a K-12 and college school, two products Ridgewell runs itself), and start an inquiry. Published company facts, the original logo and the published contact address are the evidence base. No outcomes, metrics or client identities are invented.

## Visual direction

This reads as a software company's page, not a brochure and not a ledger. Hierarchy comes from screen size, type size, whitespace and neutral rules. The mood is light and confident. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

**Colour.** The page canvas, all main sections, footer, cards, panels, header and form fields are white, `#fff`. The page, band and browser-chrome surface tokens all resolve to white, including selected sample navigation, vacant units and wireframe regions. Neutral rules separate the "Our products" section from its neighbours; outlined wireframe regions remain legible without tinted fills. Ink is `#171c22`, secondary ink `#565f68` and meta text `#636b73`; all three pass 4.5:1 on white. Ridgewell red `#b51226` (hover `#9c0f20`) is the only accent. It marks primary buttons, the 6px square before section labels, list bullets, index numerals, "What changed:" lead-ins, the focus ring and link hover. Apart from those small squares, red is a background only on the primary button, the skip link, selected text, and the buttons and logo mark inside drawn screens. Rules are neutral grey `#dedede` on the page and `#e8e8e8` inside white panels. Browser chrome is white, with neutral `#cfcfcf` dots. Inside drawn screens only, status text may also use green `#3f7a2f` and amber `#9a6210`, both at least 4.5:1 on white. Control borders, secondary button borders, the scrollbar thumb and vacant-unit outlines share `#7c858d`, with 3.75:1 contrast on white; the scrollbar track is white. These stronger edges identify controls and unit status, while the lighter rules separate content. There are no other colours, no dark bands, no gradients, no blur and no glass.

**Typography.** Barlow Condensed 600, letter-spacing -0.01em, sets h1 (48 to 88px, line-height 0.95), h2 (36 to 60px), h3 (26 to 32px), the red index numerals (24px) and the About fact values (28 to 36px). Manrope Variable sets everything else: body copy at 17px with 1.55 line-height, card copy and list items at 16px, section labels at 13px 600 uppercase with 0.08em tracking, and meta lines at 13px. Body copy never drops below 16px and captions never below 13px. There are no serif, italic display or decorative faces.

**Colour scheme.** The page is locked to the light theme. `:root` sets `color-scheme: light`, and `index.html` carries `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">` plus a white `theme-color`. Form controls set an explicit white background, ink text and `color-scheme: light`, so a dark operating-system theme does not recolour the page.

**Layout.** Content sits in a 1200px wrap with a 24 to 48px gutter, and sections are spaced 72 to 112px apart. The bookkeeping section sits between Accounting and Ways to work: the story is on the left from 960px, and two flat browser frames (a receipt chat, then the VAT and non-VAT sheets) sit on the right. Below that width the story comes first and the frames follow. It is not a card grid. The sticky white header carries the logo, centred nav (Services, Work, Approach, Contact) and a red "Start a project" button; below 960px a Menu toggle replaces the nav. The centred hero has a label, h1, lead and two buttons above a 1120px browser frame holding the units grid, with the frame's bottom clipped by the proof line. From 1024px a 200x420 phone frame showing the parent portal overlaps the frame's bottom-right corner. The proof line has one sentence and three plain fact labels separated by neutral rules. Services are two equal cards, each ending in a drawn mini UI clipped at the card's bottom edge. Accounting uses full-width ruled rows because its entries are text-only services: title and description sit alongside each other when two 18rem columns fit, and stack when space is tighter or text is enlarged. Work features card 01 across the row (screen 7/12, copy 5/12 from 768px) above a 2x2 grid of cards with 16:10 screens. The two product cards sit in a white section bounded by neutral rules, each with a screen bleeding off its bottom edge. Approach has three numbered columns, About sits copy left and facts right, Questions are a single 720px column, Contact is one white panel split into details and form, and the footer is white above a top rule.

**Components.** Section labels pair a 6px red square with the 13px uppercase label. Buttons have a 6px radius and a 48px minimum height (about 51px as rendered); the header's "Start a project" button is the compact variant at 44px. Primary is red on white text, secondary is white with a `#7c858d` border. Text links carry an arrow and turn red on hover. Cards are white, flat, 1px `#dedede`, with a 10px radius and 24 to 32px padding; they group software previews, assessment comparisons and the contact form. The browser frame has a 36px chrome strip with three 8px dots and an address bar, a 1px border, a 12px radius and the soft shadow `0 24px 60px -24px rgba(23, 28, 34, 0.18)`; the flat variant used inside cards has no shadow. The phone frame is 200x420 with a 32px radius and an 8px ink bezel. Proof labels retain 13px 600 type and list semantics without capsule borders or rounding, so facts do not suggest selectable controls. They stack with horizontal separators below 600px and sit side by side with vertical separators above that width. Accounting rows use 24px vertical padding and neutral horizontal rules to separate services without repeating the preview cards. Every interactive element is at least 44px tall, and the focus ring is a 3px red outline.

**Imagery.** Screens look like real product UI: a units grid, a parent portal, a grades table, an attendance register, an enrollment queue, an approval form, a subject enlistment, a cheques worklist, a receipt-to-ledger line, a website wireframe and a records table. They are drawn in HTML and CSS with obviously fictional names, cropped to a region and clipped by their frame, and exposed to assistive technology as one image with a descriptive accessible name. A real, tightly cropped screenshot with no personal data can replace any of the seven case-study screens through `CaseStudy.image` in `src/content.ts`. The hero's units grid and phone, and the two service screens, are always drawn: `Hero` and `Services` pass no image, so swapping them would need an `image` field added to `hero` and `services`.

**Deliberate deviations from the approved draft.** Inputs keep a visible `#7c858d` border for contrast. Meta text is `#636b73` and screen status colours are darkened so that every visible text passes 4.5:1. Browser-chrome dots are neutral rather than coloured. The phone frame is 200x420 rather than 280x580. The "Expiring in 30 days" panel sits left of the unit grid so that the phone cannot cover it. Form field labels keep their more descriptive wording, and the contact address is the published one, not the draft's placeholder.

**Do not.** No shadows except on the hero browser frame and the phone, no gradient slabs, no icon grids, no testimonial carousels, no invented metrics, no logos of other companies and no stock photography.

**Content rule.** No visible text, drawn screen, alt text or accessible name names a framework, language, database, cloud, cluster, identity provider, automation tool, module code or school-form code. Clients are described, never named: "a K-12 and college school" and "a condominium property manager". Case-study copy says who uses each system and what got easier, never how it is built. `src/lib/forbidden.ts` holds the enforced word list.

## Behavioral contract

- Existing services, approach, about, questions, and contact anchors remain functional.
- Each of the two service cards has a 'Talk about this service' link that preselects the inquiry interest.
- Ways to work presents five ordered steps and a separate privacy panel; discovery-call links reach Contact with "Not sure yet" selected.
- FAQ uses native disclosures.
- Inquiry validates locally and creates a draft for the visitor's email app. It never claims that a message has already been sent.
- Copy supports pending, success, and permission-denied states; stale completions cannot affect revised drafts.
- Mobile navigation fits short landscape viewports and scrolls internally. Escape closes it and restores focus.
- Core copy is bundled locally. No fabricated outcomes, fees, client identities, team portraits, or performance statistics.
- The bookkeeping section describes only the configured receipt agent: chat intake, VAT and non-VAT sheets in the firm's OneDrive workbook, questions when a receipt is unclear, and review by an accountant. Sample rows use fictional suppliers, peso amounts and the TIN 000-000-000-000. The mark in the favicon is the mountain emblem from the Ridgewell logo, filled with `#b51226`.
- The historical reference is accounting experience since 2004, as published by Ridgewell. It is not described as the company's founding date.

## Runtime mapping

- `src/styles.css`: tokens, type, layout, sections, responsive behaviour.
- `src/components/screens.css`: browser and phone frames, drawn product screens.
- `src/components/inquiry.css`: the inquiry form and draft states.
- `src/content.ts`: the page copy (hero, proof, services, case studies, approach, about, questions, contact details) and the inquiry options. Some copy lives in components: the Services and Questions section labels and headings, the Contact terms and the "What changed:" lead-in, the header and footer, and the form's labels, states and messages (`InquiryForm.tsx`, with validation messages in `src/lib/inquiry.ts`). `src/lib/forbidden.ts`: what copy may never say; the unit scan reads `content.ts` and the drawn screens, and the rendered-page scan in `tests/work.spec.ts` reads everything.
- `src/components/Screen.tsx` and `src/components/screens/`: the drawn screens and the slot a real screenshot replaces.

## Motion implementation

`src/hooks/useMotion.ts` owns all motion through one GSAP `matchMedia` context scoped by React's `useGSAP` and reverted on unmount. Motion runs only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. h1 and h2 headings settle 12px when they enter the viewport. The hero stage rises 24px on load, as a transform only and with no scroll trigger, so it also settles on short phone viewports where its top starts below the trigger line. Every other `[data-reveal]` card fades in and settles 12px once as it enters the viewport. Each tween clears its inline transform and opacity when it finishes. There is no parallax, no pinning and no scroll hijacking, and drawn screens never move on their own. Under reduced motion, or when the preference changes while the page is open, the context reverts and the page shows its final state with no inline transform or opacity. Sources: [GSAP React lifecycle guidance](https://gsap.com/resources/React/) and [ScrollTrigger documentation](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/).
